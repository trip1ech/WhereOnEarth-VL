## 4. Known Technical Bottlenecks & Next Directions

1. **Legacy Pairwise Ranker Collapse (Resolved in Stage 10):** The original [train_ranker.py](scripts/train_ranker.py) added unconstrained pairwise `softplus(neg - pos)` deltas onto narrow-range cosine similarities without full Top-100 listwise normalization, causing Epoch-1 collapse. Fixed in [train_geo_ranker.py](scripts/train_geo_ranker.py) and [encode_and_train_streetclip_25k.py](scripts/encode_and_train_streetclip_25k.py) via scale-normalized features, full-gallery Geo-Projection with hard same-creator negatives, spatial KDE, and Listwise Softmax KL.
2. **VLM Coordinate SFT Diminishing Returns:** [sft_colab.py](scripts/sft_colab.py) optimizes unweighted token cross-entropy over 3-decimal lat/lon strings without spherical distance geometry (a wrong hemisphere sign costs 1 token), and 512 px resizing blurs distant signs and markings.
3. **2B Parameter-Memory Ceiling for Toponyms:** Case studies proved that even with perfect human transcripts (`POINTE DES CHATEAUX`, `BOUAKÉ`), a 2B model cannot reliably map mid-/long-tail place names to coordinates — motivating external offline gazetteer lookup (GeoNames) gated by OCR confidence.
4. **Single-Adapter Multi-Task Interference:** Continuing 6k LoRA on mixed clue + localization tasks caused task dilution (direct localization dropped `33 → 30/66`) and over-conservative OCR abstention. Clue extraction and geolocalization should remain decoupled adapters.
5. **Pinpoint (≤ 25 km) Gap vs. 5M `osv5m/baseline`:** Wide-kernel KDE (`σ = 500 km`) maximizes GeoGuessr score at the expense of pinpoint ≤ 25 km hits. Scaling the `StreetCLIP` gallery beyond 25k (e.g., 100k+) and adopting two-stage coarse-to-fine KDE (`σ = 500 km` region → `σ = 30–50 km` local refinement) is the clearest lever to close the ≤ 25 km gap.
